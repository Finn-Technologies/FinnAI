# FinnAI Google Play Listing (en-US)

## App name

Finn AI: Local AI Assistant

## Short description

Private on-device AI assistant. No account needed. Chat offline.

## Full description

Meet FinnAI, a fast and private AI assistant designed to run locally on your
Android device.

Your conversations and AI processing stay on your phone. For news, recipes,
and other requests that need web information, FinnAI sends only the latest
request as a search query and shows tappable sources. FinnAI automatically checks
your device's memory and processor capabilities, then installs a compact
on-device model tuned for smooth, private answers.

Use FinnAI to:

- Ask questions and continue natural multi-turn conversations
- Draft, explain, summarize, brainstorm, and reason without a cloud AI service
- Turn ideas into saved Creations: widgets, mini apps and presentations
- Set up Flows that run on a schedule, like a morning brief
- Choose what FinnAI remembers in You, and review it any time
- Search your chats, creations and memories in one place
- Keep conversation history encrypted in app-private local storage
- Export, delete or erase conversations, the model, memories and other local data from Settings

FinnAI needs at least 3 GB of RAM and a 64-bit ARM Android device. The initial
AI model download requires an internet connection. AI responses are generated
on device after installation.

AI can make mistakes. Verify important medical, legal, financial, or safety
information independently.
